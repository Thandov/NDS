Delicious Chicken & Mushroom Dish

Cooking Time: 30 mins | Prep Time: 10 mins | Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 50g button mushrooms
  • 25g butter
  • 30g red pimento (skinned)
  • 300g cooked boiled chicken
  • 10ml Sherry
  • 125ml chicken veloute (chicken stock)
  • 15ml cream or non-dairy cream

Steps

  1. Wash and slice the mushrooms.
  2. Cook them without color in the butter.
  3. If using raw pimento, discard the seeds, cut into dice, and cook with the mushrooms.
  4. Cut the chicken into small, neat slices.
  5. Add the chicken to the mushrooms and pimento.
  6. Drain off the fat. Add the sherry.
  7. Add the veloute, bring to a boil.
  8. Finish with the cream and correct the seasoning.
  9. Place into a serving dish and decorate with small strips of cooked pimento.
